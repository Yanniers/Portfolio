A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Learning, creating, and growing.

I first started building in Roblox Studio in 2018, but had to step away in 2019. After taking a long break, I've recently returned to building and creating again.

I'm now focused on improving my skills, learning new techniques, and developing my own style as a builder. I enjoy working on environments, structures, and interiors, especially projects where I can bring a concept to life through layout, colors, and details.

This portfolio is a collection of my current work and progress. I'm always open to learning, experimenting, and taking on new creative challenges.

I am now building for filipino games, so you might notice the styles of my buildings are a bit different from usuals build for simulator games or any other games.

I'm currently rebuilding my experience one project at a time — with a focus on improving, learning, and making each build better than the last.
